# Candidate Profile

## Stable facts

- 2026 Master of Science graduate from National Tsing Hua University's Institute of Information Systems and Applications (ISA).
- Malaysian citizen.
- Singapore employment requires employer-supported EP or S Pass.
- Mainland China employment requires employer-supported foreign work permit.
- Hong Kong employment normally requires an employer-supported General Employment Policy (GEP) route for this candidate.
- Malaysia employment does not require foreign-work-permit sponsorship for this candidate.
- MyCareersFuture (MCF) is not a usable application channel for this candidate. An SG role that is MCF-only is blocked unless a separate verified non-MCF application route exists.

## Positioning

Early-career software engineer with applied-AI strength.

Primary role families:

- Software Engineer / SDE
- Backend Engineer
- Platform / Infrastructure / Systems
- Data / Search / Retrieval engineering
- ML Systems / AI Platform / Inference / Evaluation
- Applied AI / LLM / RAG / Agent engineering

Do not require an `AI Engineer` title. Prefer strong software-engineering career capital over novelty of title.

## First-job preference

The immediate goal is interviews and offers for a suitable **permanent full-time** engineering job, normally on-site or hybrid.

Prioritize:

- production engineering ownership;
- backend/API/data/cloud/distributed-systems depth;
- code review and strong engineering peers;
- deployment, reliability, testing and observability;
- mentorship and a credible engineering ladder;
- portable skills and employer/team reputation;
- applied-AI work when the engineering environment is also strong.

Applied AI is a differentiator, not a substitute for engineering quality.

For the main first-job lane, permanent employment is preferred. Fixed-term contract, staffing/bodyshop or project-only employment should not be promoted unless the role is unusually strong and the user explicitly accepts that exact exception. Web3 remains a separate opportunistic lane where contract/part-time/project work can still be useful.

Keep using the current resume and project evidence. Change them only for a concrete application need or repeated relevant employer feedback. Do not restart portfolio polishing as a substitute for applications.

## Malaysia TOP definition

Malaysia is an active parallel lane, but **TOP is role-level, not logo-level**.

Prefer roles with a strong combination of:

- credible engineering/product environment;
- meaningful backend / platform / AI engineering depth;
- good compensation and reasonable WLB when evidence exists;
- mentorship, growth and career portability;
- strong fit with the candidate's actual evidence.

A famous bank, GLC, consultancy or MNC name alone does not make a role TOP. Generic operations, low-depth IT support, bodyshop/outsourcing or weak consulting implementation roles should not displace better engineering opportunities.

## Opportunistic Web3 work

Credible Web3 / crypto engineering work may be considered as a secondary lane when it provides useful engineering experience, income or network value. This can include full-time, part-time, contract, remote or project-based work, especially backend/API, infrastructure, wallet/custody, exchange/trading infrastructure, developer tooling/SDK, data/indexing, or AI × Web3 engineering.

This does **not** replace the SG/CN/HK + MY TOP first-job search. Detailed Web3 quality, safety, freshness and dedupe rules belong in `RULES.md`, not here.

## Seniority

- Mandatory `3+ years` is a meaningful negative signal, not an automatic reject.
- Skip Senior / Staff / Lead / Manager and hard 5+ YOE requirements unless there is an explicit graduate/early-career exception.

## Search markets

### Active target: Singapore

Search Singapore actively across strong early-career SWE/backend/platform/AI roles.

- Unknown sponsorship/pass willingness is allowed at APPLY.
- Explicit no-sponsorship/right-to-work-only wording is a blocker.
- MCF-only application routes are blocked.
- Prefer employers/roles where compensation and hiring structure make EP/S Pass support realistic when evidence is available, but do not require public sponsorship wording before applying.

### Active target: Mainland China

Search Mainland China actively, especially strong product, AI-native, enterprise AI, developer-tool, search/retrieval, platform and Agent/RAG teams.

- Distinguish legal work-permit plausibility from employer willingness.
- Employer willingness may remain UNKNOWN until engagement; UNKNOWN alone must not prevent an otherwise strong, live, eligible role from reaching APPLY.
- Favor 2026-compatible, recent-graduate, 0–3 YOE and strong 2–4 YOE stretch opportunities.

### Active target: Hong Kong

Hong Kong is in scope for strong engineering, AI, fintech, digital-bank, product and research-to-product roles.

- The normal work-authorization route is employer-supported GEP.
- Unknown GEP sponsorship willingness is allowed at APPLY.
- Explicit local-right-to-work / no-sponsorship wording is a blocker.
- Do not use TTPS or another unrelated immigration path as a substitute for verifying the employer route.
- Prefer permanent full-time roles. Fixed-term research appointments require an explicit role-level exception decision before APPLY.

### Parallel lane: Malaysia TOP only

Malaysia runs in parallel because there is no foreign-work-permit friction, but do not fill the queue with ordinary fallback jobs.

Prefer AI Engineer / Applied AI / AI Software Engineer / LLM / RAG / Agent / AI backend-platform roles and strong SWE/backend/platform jobs at genuinely high-quality employers/teams.

Do not spend search time verifying Malaysian sponsorship. Normal role fit, liveness, company/team quality, dedupe and application-route checks still apply.

Employers with meaningful regional engineering exposure are a plus, but do not assume future internal transfer without evidence.

## Review trigger after 2026-10-31

After 2026-10-31, reassess market weighting based on **substantive progression**: human recruiter screens/interviews, OA followed by real next-round movement, or comparable employer engagement.

If SG/CN/HK remain weak while Malaysia produces stronger real traction, increase MY TOP application weight. Do not automatically abandon SG/CN/HK because of one rejection or short-term silence.
