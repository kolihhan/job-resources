Soft/preferred stack gaps are not hard blockers.

## First-job preference

The immediate goal is to maximize realistic interviews and offers for a **permanent full-time** engineering job, normally on-site or hybrid.

Prioritize stronger opportunities first when available:

- production engineering ownership;
- Python/backend/API/database work;
- Agent/RAG/retrieval/evaluation systems;
- deployment, reliability, testing and observability around AI applications;
- code review and strong engineering peers;
- mentorship and a credible engineering ladder;
- portable skills and employer/team reputation;
- applied-AI work with real engineering depth rather than prompt/demo-only work;
- low-friction/direct application routes when role quality and fit are otherwise comparable.

These determine **ordering**, not whether a plausible early-career role is allowed into the queue.

For the current job search, **permanent full-time is a hard filter**. Fixed-term, temporary, contract, trainee-without-confirmed-permanent-employment, staffing/bodyshop and project-only employment should be skipped/closed rather than promoted.

Keep using the current resume and project evidence. Change them only for a concrete application need or repeated relevant employer feedback. Do not restart portfolio polishing as a substitute for applications.

## Company quality

Company/team quality is a prioritization factor, not a baseline eligibility rule.

Prefer roles with a strong combination of:

- credible engineering/product environment;
- meaningful technical ownership;
- good compensation and reasonable WLB when evidence exists;
- mentorship, growth and career portability;
- strong fit with the candidate's actual evidence.

But ordinary credible employers may still enter APPLY when the role is live, permanent, early-career realistic and technically plausible. Do not require a TOP-company label before creating an opportunity.

## Seniority

- Prefer new graduate / 0–3 years roles.
- 1–3 YOE is normal target/stretch territory.
- A reasonable 2–4 YOE role may survive when the hard requirements and actual ownership are still plausible; it does not need to be an exceptional company merely to be considered.
- Mandatory `3+ years` is a negative signal, not an automatic rejection when the wording is generic software-engineering experience that can truthfully include paid part-time/intern production work.
- Hard 4+/5+ professional-YOE floors normally skip unless the JD explicitly provides an alternative route the candidate truly satisfies.
- A `Senior` title is a strong review flag, **not an automatic terminal rule**. Read the JD: if it has a hard mature-YOE floor or mature ownership, skip; if the actual requirements remain early-career realistic, it may survive as a stretch.
- `Staff`, `Principal`, `Lead`, `Manager`, `Director` and clearly mature enterprise/platform/customer ownership are default skips.
- Specialized professional-experience floors matter: e.g. `2+ years professional AI application/service development` should not be satisfied by academic/project AI work plus unrelated software employment.

## Search markets

Search Singapore broadly for early-career engineering opportunities, with Applied AI / Agent / RAG / evaluation / AI backend first in priority and general SWE/backend/full-stack/test automation as active lanes.

- Unknown sponsorship/pass willingness is allowed at APPLY.
- Explicit no-sponsorship/right-to-work-only wording is a blocker.
- MCF-only application routes are blocked.
- Prefer employers/roles where compensation and hiring structure make EP/S Pass support realistic when evidence is available, but do not require public sponsorship wording before applying.
- Do not reject an otherwise plausible role solely because it is not AI.

### Active target: Mainland China

Search Mainland China broadly across 2026-compatible software engineering, backend, test-development and AI application roles.

- Distinguish legal work-permit plausibility from employer willingness.
- Employer willingness may remain UNKNOWN until engagement; UNKNOWN alone must not prevent an otherwise live, eligible role from reaching APPLY.
- Favor 2026-compatible, recent-graduate and 0–3 YOE opportunities.
- Reasonable 2–4 YOE stretches may survive when the hard requirements and scope remain realistic.
- Native recruitment sources remain especially important for coverage.

### Paused: Hong Kong

Hong Kong is **paused as of 2026-10-23**.

- Do not discover new HK roles.
- Do not promote new HK APPLY/ACTION items until the user explicitly reactivates HK.
- Existing submitted `WAIT` items remain open only so employer replies are not lost.
- If an existing HK employer replies, process that lifecycle event normally.
- Roles closed only because HK was paused may be reconsidered later, but must be freshly revalidated after reactivation.

### Active target: Malaysia

Malaysia is an active broad engineering lane because there is no foreign-work-permit friction.

Prefer TOP companies/teams and Applied AI / AI Software Engineer / LLM / RAG / Agent / evaluation / AI backend roles, but those are **priorities rather than gates**.

General SWE/backend/full-stack/test-development and other realistically compatible early-career engineering roles may reach APPLY when live, permanent and truthfully applicable. Do not spend search time verifying Malaysian sponsorship.

Employers with meaningful regional engineering exposure are a plus, but do not assume future internal transfer without evidence.
